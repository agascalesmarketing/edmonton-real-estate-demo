SELLING · A practical guide

A considered selling checklist.

Prepare your home, your documents, and your next move without the overwhelm.

In this guide

A few clear steps.

  1. Gather the story of your home
  2. Make space for first impressions
  3. Discuss a pricing approach
  4. Think through the transition

General education only. Consult qualified professionals for advice about your circumstances.

01

Gather the story of your home.

Collect renovation records, permits, warranties, and relevant maintenance notes. Ask a licensed professional what disclosures and documents apply to your property.

02

Make space for first impressions.

Focus on cleaning, simple repairs, lighting, and uncluttered rooms before committing to expensive upgrades. Evaluate changes against your timeline and budget.

03

Discuss a pricing approach.

A price strategy should be informed by current, verified comparable properties and your circumstances. This demo provides no valuation, market analysis, or estimate of sale proceeds.

04

Think through the transition.

Coordinate your next home, possession dates, moving arrangements, and costs. Talk with your lawyer about contractual obligations before relying on a particular timeline.

North & Nest is a fictional portfolio demonstration. This guide does not provide professional advice or establish current rates, eligibility, regulations, or market conditions.
