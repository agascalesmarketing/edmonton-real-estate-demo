RELOCATION · A practical guide

Make Edmonton feel like home.

A practical way to explore the city and compare what matters to your household.

In this guide

A few clear steps.

  1. Map your daily life
  2. Explore neighbourhoods in person
  3. Verify services directly
  4. Plan for the seasons

General education only. Consult qualified professionals for advice about your circumstances.

01

Map your daily life.

List work locations, recurring activities, and the places you visit most. Compare actual routes at relevant travel times instead of relying on a single map estimate.

02

Explore neighbourhoods in person.

Spend time on several streets and visit at different times of day. Compare housing types, your comfort with maintenance, and the rhythm of each setting.

03

Verify services directly.

Check school boundaries and enrolment with the relevant school boards, transit with ETS, and municipal information with the City of Edmonton. Details can change.

04

Plan for the seasons.

Include heating, winter maintenance, and seasonal moving conditions in your planning. Ask questions about a home’s insulation, systems, and maintenance history.

North & Nest is a fictional portfolio demonstration. This guide does not provide professional advice or establish current rates, eligibility, regulations, or market conditions.
