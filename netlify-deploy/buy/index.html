The steps below are general education. For an actual purchase, work with appropriately licensed professionals.

01

Define your home.

List the spaces, location preferences, and everyday routines that matter. Separate essentials from nice-to-haves.

02

Know your budget.

Discuss financing with a qualified lender. Include closing costs, ongoing expenses, and a maintenance cushion.

03

Explore with care.

Compare homes and neighbourhoods. Ask about condition, documents, permitted use, and address-specific details.

04

Make an informed move.

Understand contract conditions and timelines. Consult a licensed professional and a lawyer before committing.
