INVESTING · A practical guide

Look beyond the asking price.

Questions to help you evaluate costs, responsibilities, and risk.

In this guide

A few clear steps.

  1. Build a realistic cost picture
  2. Understand your responsibilities
  3. Investigate the property
  4. Keep room for uncertainty

General education only. Consult qualified professionals for advice about your circumstances.

01

Build a realistic cost picture.

Consider financing, taxes, insurance, repairs, management, vacancy, and any condo fees. Use verified property-specific numbers rather than promised returns.

02

Understand your responsibilities.

Review current Alberta landlord and tenant requirements through official provincial resources. Verify permitted use, zoning, and any building or association rules before purchasing.

03

Investigate the property.

Ask about condition, ongoing maintenance, applicable permits, and condominium documentation. Discuss tax and legal implications with qualified advisers.

04

Keep room for uncertainty.

Test how vacancy, higher costs, or an unexpected repair would affect your budget. This concept offers no income projection, investment recommendation, or guarantee.

North & Nest is a fictional portfolio demonstration. This guide does not provide professional advice or establish current rates, eligibility, regulations, or market conditions.
