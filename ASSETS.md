# Asset provenance

## Architectural images

All six architectural images were generated specifically for this fictional portfolio using OpenAI image generation. They are stored locally as optimized WebP files in `src/assets/images/` and copied into both output folders. They are fictional, not photographs of actual Edmonton homes or neighbourhoods. Each use includes meaningful alt text; property listings, neighbourhood imagery, and the demonstration page explain the illustrative nature of the images.

- `glenora.webp`: contemporary cedar-and-stone home and garden; homepage hero and fictional Glenora property.
- `ritchie.webp`: character-inspired home with covered porch and garden.
- `windermere.webp`: contemporary luxury residence with stone and cedar.
- `strathcona.webp`: urban brick low-rise condominium concept.
- `summerside.webp`: prairie-inspired contemporary family home.
- `townhome.webp`: brick-and-charcoal contemporary townhomes.

Generated source PNGs were converted to WebP at quality 86 for delivery. No external image hotlinks, stock image accounts, scraped listings, or API credentials are required.

## Fonts

Cormorant Garamond (display) and Manrope (body) are bundled locally. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. License texts are included beside the fonts and in both output folders.

Authoritative source files:

- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope

Downloaded from the Google Fonts repository using HTTPS with normal TLS verification. No runtime requests are made to Google Fonts or other font services.

## Logo and icons

The North & Nest architectural mark, favicon, and SVG arrows are original inline/vector artwork created for the concept. They do not represent a real brokerage identity.
